Yuliang Guo is a Principal Scientist at Maven Robotics, where he works on general-purpose robotic AI for complex industrial tasks, focusing on vision-language-action generalization and robotic learning from physical experience. Previously, he was a Lead Research Scientist at Bosch Research in Sunnyvale, CA (2021–2025), leading research in unified 3D vision, scalable neural reconstruction, closed-loop simulation, and physical AI across industrial AR, robot navigation and manipulation, and autonomous parking. Earlier in his career, he was a Senior Research Scientist at OPPO Research (2019–2020), working on 3D perception and human pose estimation for AR, and a Senior Research Engineer at Baidu Apollo (2018–2019), developing 3D perception systems for autonomous driving. He received his Ph.D. in Computer Science from Brown University in 2018, advised by Benjamin Kimia and Thomas Serre, and his B.S. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University. His research has been published in premier computer vision, machine learning, and robotics venues including CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, ICLR, NeurIPS, ICRA, IROS, and TPAMI.
